# PramaanFlow Design System

## Overview
A clean, modern, and trustworthy interface for a government compliance and regulatory platform. The design emphasizes clarity, structured data presentation, and clear status indication.

## Colors
* **Primary:** Teal (#14b8a6) - Used for primary actions, success states, and active indicators.
* **Neutral/Surface:** Slate (#f8fafc to #0f172a) - Used for backgrounds, text, and structural borders.
* **Accent (Warning):** Amber (#f59e0b) - Used for pending actions and warnings.
* **Accent (Error):** Red (#ef4444) - Used for failed states or critical alerts.
* **Background:** Very light slate (`#f8fafc`) with white (`#ffffff`) cards.

## Typography
* **Font Family:** Inter / System Sans-Serif.
* **Headings:** Bold to Extrabold. Use dark slate (`text-slate-900`).
* **Micro-copy / Section Headers:** Tiny, bold, uppercase with wide tracking (e.g., 10px, `tracking-wider`, `text-slate-500`).

## Components
* **Cards:** White background, subtle border (`border-slate-200`), small shadow (`shadow-sm`), large rounded corners (`rounded-xl`).
* **Buttons:**
  * Primary: Teal background, slate-900 text, bold, slightly rounded (`rounded-lg`).
  * Secondary: White background, slate border, slate text, hover states.
* **Forms:** Light slate inputs (`bg-slate-50`), rounded edges, teal focus rings.
* **Status Badges:** Small pill shapes or inline text with corresponding status colors (Teal for success, Amber for pending, Slate for locked).

## Layout
* **Spacing:** Generous padding inside cards (p-5, p-6).
* **Navigation:** Sidebar for task navigation (w-80), top navigation for global context and roles.
